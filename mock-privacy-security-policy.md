Sentara RMH Wellness Center is under the Sentara Healthcare corporation.
These Policies ONLY apply to the employees of Sentara RMH Wellness
Center. If the employee is medical staff they are under the Policy of
the Sentara RMH Hospital, not the Sentara RMH Wellness Center Policy.

ALL SENTARA EMPLOYEES HAVE READ AND SIGNED A HIPAA CONTRACT AND ARE
EXPECTED TO FOLLOW HIPAA LAWS AT ALL TIMES.

Sentara RMH Wellness Center employees have no access to EPIC healthcare
or any Sentara Patient information.

## I. Employee Technology Usage

### A. Internet

1.  All internet usage whether private network or guest network will be
    monitored at all times by Sentara.

2.  All Sentara issued devices must be on the private wellness center
    wifi. All Sentara issued desktops computers must be connected to the
    private WiFi through ethernet connection. The private WiFi muse has
    WPA2 security and no less bandwidth then 5G. No personal employee
    devices are to be contented to the WiFi, if an unauthorized device
    is on the center WiFi it will be removed.

    a.  Software and code downloads from the internet are NOT permitted
        and will be logged as an Unauthorized Action. All Sentara issue
        devices will not allow these downloads any tampering with the
        admin permissions on the device to allow these downloads will be
        investigated by Sentara.

3.  Any personal device (laptop, Ipad, phone, WiFi compatible watch,
    etc) must be connected to the Guest WiFi, as well as any device a
    member has access to such as a TV in the gym. The Guest WiFi must
    have WPA2 security and no more bandwidth then 2.5G. No device may
    have automatic connection capabilities to this network.

    a.  If a site is blocked on Sentara private WiFi DO NOT disconnect
        Sentara computers from the private WiFi connection. Contact the
        IT Help Support for help with connecting.

### B. Login Process

1.  All Sentara issued devices will be preimaged with Sentara API. To
    login, use employee Workday login and provide dual authentication
    from third party source DUO.

2.  NO SHARING OF ACCOUNT INFORMATION. Employees will be held
    accountable for any activity on the employees account login.

    a.  If it was unauthorized access to the employees account they must
        report the unauthorized request for the dual authentication
        within 24 hours to the IT Help Support or be held responsible
        for any activity that has accrued on account.

    b.  If an employee has been found to have given unauthorized access
        to an individual to their account they will be instigated.
        Sentara has the right to sue and revoke license for HIPAA
        Violations. Sentara has the right to sue within the boundaries
        of the law.

3.  Password Policy:

    a.  All passwords must be 8 characters.\
    b.  One upper case, one lower case, and at least one special
        character, and one number.\
    c.  No Birthdays\
    d.  No Names\
    e.  No repeats of the last 6 passwords by the employee.\
    f.  Passwords must be updated every 6 months, notice will be sent
        out 2 weeks before password expiration.

4.  All Sentara employees must be registered to DUO, Okta or another
    dual authentication generator to log in to any Sentara device or
    account.

5.  No logged in computer MAY BE UNATTENDED. If devices are left
    unattended and unattended access occurs, logged in employees will be
    held responsible by negligence.

### C. Personal Accounts

1.  No access to personal email, google, microsoft or any other account
    is permitted on a Sentara issued device.

    a.  If found logged in to personal accounts on Sentara device, the
        employee will be logged out of the account and a violation will
        be added to employee's Workday.

    b.  Continued violation of this rule will lead to the employee's
        probation, and if the violation continues, loss of employment.

### D. Insertable devices

1.  No unauthorized devices may be placed in the computer this includes:

    a.  USBs\
    b.  Phone chargers\
    c.  Keyboards (not issued by Sentara)\
    d.  Mice (not issued by Sentara)\
    e.  Medical devices (Not issued by Sentara)\
    f.  Any other non approved Sentara issued devices

2.  For any questions about allowed devices contact IT Support Help. If
    an employee has a device that they must use that is on this list
    they must get written authorization from IT Support to place devices
    into the computer.

### F. Video Chat and Calls

1.  No video chats such as FaceType, Skype, or Zoom, may take place in a

## II. Music policy

### A.

All music played in the RMH Wellness Center by group instructors, staff
or for any event held in the Wellness center must follow U.S Congress
Copyright Law.

1.  All music must be properly licensed through use of a Business
    account.
2.  Instructors for group lessons must use business, group lesson
    approved and followed copyrighted music for playlists.
3.  Sentara has the right to sue employees for any Copyright Violation
    that may occur.
4.  Any questions about approved music sites or licenses may be directed
    to the Sentara Legal team.

### B.

All music must be radio safe, no explicit songs may be played. If
disputes arise over song selections or inappropriate content, the
Wellness Center is expected to immediately change the music or song.

1.  ALL ages are allowed in the Center, keep this in mind while
    selecting songs.

## III. Personal Information Privacy

### A.

Members must provide the following personal information at time of
membership creation: Name, address, email, age, date of birth, and pre
existing health condition we must be aware of.

1.  This information is for administrative purposes only and will be
    used for billing and updating members on Wellness center news.
2.  Members' photos will also be stored in
    https://rmh.clubautomation.com/calendar/programs along with their
    name, age and if applicable gender, only for identification purposes
    at login.

### B.

Sentara will not release any information about members unless a member
has signed a waiver allowing the release of information to specific
individuals. Waivers must include members name, birthdate and member
number, with allowed individuals name and relationship to member with
signatures from, the member and the General Manager of the Wellness
center.

### C.

Sentara DOES NOT SELL MEMBER INFORMATION TO THIRD PARTIES. No member
information such as age, name, or email will be given to a third party
for financial gain.

### D.

Sentara does allow for non-identifiable information within the
constraints of HIPAA to be given for research purposes regarding
equipment usages and finance studies. Members have the right to at any
time during membership to sign waivers to be exempt from any information
being given for research purposes.

### E.

For the purpose of Personal trainers and Swim instructors, age, name and
gender will be provided to them on schedule confirmation of the member.
This will ONLY be used by instructors to make fitness plans.

1.  This information will be stored on rmhcluautomation.com and only
    accessible to the personal trainer and swim instructor.

### F.

ANY EMPLOYEE THAT VIOLATES OUR PRIVACY POLICY WILL BE HELD ACCOUNTABLE
BY SNETAR AND ARE ALSO ABLE TO BE PROSECUTED IN VIRGINIA COURTS FOR
VIOLATION OF VIRGINIA PRIVACY LAW VIOLATION AS WELL AS FEDERAL COURT FOR
HIPAA VIOLATION.

## IV. Safely and Certification Policy

### A.

All Sentara employees must have valid and up-to-date CPR and first aid
training certification.

### B.

All members must provide emergency contact information with an
individual's name, relationship to member and phone number.

### C.

At time of membership creation or nonmember with a visitor pass/lesson
ALL must sign a implied consent form that allows Sentara employees to
take any means necessary in a emergency where a member and/or Legal
guardian is unable to provide consent, emergency actions include not
limited to:

1.  Choking Meanuevers
2.  Lifeguard Saves, including backboarding if necessary
3.  calling 911
4.  Authorizing ambulance transfer
5.  CPR

## V. Member and Employee Video and photo Policy

### A.

NO photos or videos are allowed in the RMH Wellness Center unless it is
of ONLY yourself or your child.

### B.

Any unauthorized photo taking or video usage in the RMH Wellness center
will be handled seriously.

1.  If violation of personal privacy occurs, the accused party must
    delete all detailed photos or videos that they may have of the
    violated individual.

### C.

No unauthorized photos or videos of staff members.

### D.

Violations of video and photo policy by employees may be a HIPAA
violation.

### E.

No personal devices may be used to take photos or videos in the Wellness
center by an employee. Only Sentara issued cameras with signed
permission slips from all parties being filmed are allowed to be taken
by Sentara staff.

1.  Any employee in violation must delete all content that was
    unauthorized. They will also be held accountable by Senbtara whether
    financially for damages and/or by suspension or termination of
    employment.

### F.

NO posting or video taping of swim lessons unless it is your own child.
Any posting of minors without parental consent is a crime and anyone
violating this will be held accountable by Virginia Courts.

### G.

IF sentara employee posts inappropriate content on social media or any
other platform that contains sentara logo or merchandise will be found
in CopyRight violation.

1.  They must remove the content immediately from all platforms.
2.  Employees may be held financially responsible for damages as well as
    suspended or terminated from employment by Sentara.
