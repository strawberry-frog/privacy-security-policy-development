# 🛡️📋 Privacy & Security Policy Development

**Graduate Cybersecurity Coursework --- James Madison University**

## Overview

This project focused on researching privacy, security, legal, and
ethical requirements and translating them into organizational policy
recommendations.

The work examined how an organization can collect, use, protect, and
disclose personal information while balancing operational needs with
individual privacy. It also considered safeguards for sensitive and
health-related information, employee responsibilities, access controls,
and appropriate use of organizational technology.

> **Portfolio note:** This repository is a professional summary of
> academic policy-development work. It is intended to demonstrate
> privacy, security, compliance, and policy-analysis skills rather than
> serve as legal advice or as an official policy for any organization.

## Areas of Focus

-   Privacy and protection of personal information
-   Health-information privacy and safeguards
-   Appropriate collection and use of member/customer information
-   Restrictions on disclosure or sale of personal information
-   Access control and authorized use
-   Password and account-security practices
-   Secure network and system configurations
-   Protection of sensitive organizational information
-   Organizational responsibilities for safeguarding data
-   Ethical considerations surrounding privacy and technology use

## Regulatory & Legal Research

The broader project work included research into privacy and security
requirements and frameworks such as:

-   **HIPAA**
-   **HITECH**
-   **CPNI**
-   **ECPA**
-   **COPPA**
-   **CCPA**
-   Relevant state privacy requirements

The project also examined legal considerations outside traditional
cybersecurity regulation when they affected organizational technology
policy, including copyright and public use of digital media.

## Policy Development

The project translated research into practical organizational policy
recommendations.

### Personal Information & Privacy

Policy work addressed how personal information should be collected and
used for legitimate organizational purposes while limiting unnecessary
disclosure.

Topics included:

-   appropriate organizational use of personal information,
-   restrictions on sharing information with third parties,
-   protecting individual privacy,
-   limiting information use to legitimate business purposes,
-   handling identifying information used for account or membership
    verification.

### Sensitive & Health-Related Information

Because the example organization operated in an environment connected
with healthcare services, the project considered the additional privacy
responsibilities surrounding health-related and individually
identifiable information.

This included research into HIPAA safeguards and the responsibilities
organizations and business associates may have when handling protected
information.

### Security Controls

Security recommendations included:

-   password management,
-   access controls,
-   secure network configurations,
-   protection of sensitive information,
-   limiting access to authorized users,
-   organizational safeguards for systems containing private
    information.

## Ethical Analysis

The project did not treat privacy as only a compliance problem. It also
evaluated policy choices through multiple ethical perspectives.

The analysis considered questions such as:

-   When is collecting identifying information justified?
-   How should an organization balance operational needs with individual
    privacy?
-   When does the use or disclosure of personal information become
    inappropriate?
-   What responsibilities does an organization have when it possesses
    information that could expose individuals to harm or discrimination?

This helped connect technical and legal requirements with the broader
consequences of organizational security and privacy decisions.

## What I Learned

This project strengthened my understanding that cybersecurity controls
operate within a larger organizational and regulatory environment.

Technical protections such as access controls, password requirements,
and secure network configurations are only part of protecting
information. Organizations also need clear rules defining what
information may be collected, who may access it, how it may be used,
when it may be disclosed, and what safeguards are required.

The project also reinforced the importance of writing security and
privacy requirements so that they can be understood and applied by the
people responsible for following them.

This repository highlights the policy, privacy, and compliance side of
